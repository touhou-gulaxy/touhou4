# the higher the tier, the lower the chance of success.
# also releated to the quantity of ingredients used, the more ingredients, the lower the chance of success.
# and higher the capital tier, the higher the chance of success.
# P = 1% * (K * capital_tier^1.5) / (K * capital_tier^1.5 + quantity^1.2 + tier^2), with K = 20
# worst chance is P(1, 5, 4) = 1% * (20 * 1^1.5) / (20 * 1^1.5 + 5^1.2 + 4^2)
# 							 = 1% * (20) / (20 + 6.898 + 16) = 46.62%.
